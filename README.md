## 📌 Overview

**MindMate** is a dual-purpose AI-driven platform that supports both **mental health** and **academic learning** for students.  
It combines emotional wellness tools with AI-powered study aids — offering therapy-like experiences, stress analytics, and textbook insights within a single, privacy-first ecosystem.

---

## 🎯 The Problem We Solve

### 🧠 Mental Health
- 70% of Indian students experience anxiety or depression  
- Therapy costs ₹2000+ per session and long wait times  
- Stigma and lack of access prevent early intervention  

### 📚 Academic Stress
- Textbooks are dense and hard to digest quickly  
- Students need simplified summaries and concept extraction  
- Exam preparation increases anxiety levels  

---

## 💡 Our Dual Solution

### 🌿 Mental Wellness Features
- 🥽 **VR Exposure Therapy** – Safe 3D environments for facing fears  
- 🧠 **AI Mood Analytics** – Detect emotional patterns & stress peaks  
- 🎮 **Therapeutic Games** – Clinically-backed relaxation experiences  
- 💬 **AI Companion** – 24/7 emotional support chatbot  
- 📊 **Wellness Insights** – Personalized mental health dashboard  

### 📖 Study Support Features
- 📚 **AI Textbook Analysis** – Extract chapters, headings, and key concepts  
- 🧾 **Structured Content** – Organized output for faster learning  
- 🗣️ **Text-to-Speech** – Listen to your textbooks via AI voices  
- ⚡ **Fast Processing** – Handles large PDFs efficiently  
- 📝 **Smart Summaries** – Key concept generation using NLP

## ✨ Core Modules

### 1️⃣ VR Therapy Environments
Immersive VR worlds for exposure therapy:
- Peaceful Garden 🌳 – mindfulness & relaxation  
- College Campus 🎓 – social anxiety exposure  
- Exam Hall 🧾 – test anxiety desensitization  
- Home Room 🏠 – safe guided breathing sessions  

**Tech:** Three.js • React Three Fiber • WebXR • @react-three/drei  

---

### 2️⃣ AI MindInsights Dashboard
Track and visualize emotional data:
- Morning & evening mood check-ins  
- Sleep & energy tracking  
- Stress prediction & intervention timing  
- AI insights like: _“Your mood drops every Monday”_  

**Tech:** TensorFlow.js • Brain.js • Recharts • Simple Statistics  

---

### 3️⃣ Therapeutic Mini-Games
Play scientifically-designed games for calmness:
- Breathing Rhythm Game  
- Worry Bubble Pop  
- Emotion Pattern Match  
- Mindful Maze  

**Tech:** HTML5 Canvas • Custom physics • Framer Motion  

---

### 4️⃣ AI Textbook Analyzer 📚
Instantly convert PDFs into clean, study-ready notes:
- Smart chapter & heading detection  
- Concept extraction via NLP  
- Text-to-speech playback  
- Markdown-style organized output  

**Tech:** Python • Flask • PyPDF2 • pdfplumber  

---

### 5️⃣ AI Companion Chatbot 💬
A 24/7 CBT-based support assistant:
- Emotion & sentiment detection  
- Crisis recognition system  
- Optional facial emotion detection  
- Encouraging, mood-aware conversation  

**Tech:** ML5.js • Face-API.js • Web Speech API
